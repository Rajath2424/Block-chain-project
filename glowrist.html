— The Glowrist way —

From seed to your hands

I

Choose a batch

Pick a future harvest of roses, peonies or tulips from a named grower.

II

Pay into escrow

Pay in GloomEuro. The smart contract holds your money and checks there is stock.

III

Get your NFT

A reservation NFT shows the batch, stems, origin and deadline.

IV

Grower is paid in stages

Planted 30%, harvested & shipped 40%, arrived in Dublin 30%. Each stage is checked by a verifier.

V

Collect in the shop

Sign with your wallet in store. The reservation NFT is burned and you take your flowers.

VI

Keep a keepsake

You get a new keepsake NFT with the flower picture and its origin, in your name.

If a crop fails, or the batch is not here by the deadline, you claim back the full amount from escrow.
