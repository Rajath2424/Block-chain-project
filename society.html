How it works

Four steps from approval to cash

1

Get approved

Complete KYC/AML with the credit union, connect MetaMask and sign a message to prove the wallet is yours.

2

Receive your certificate

After payment through the bank, the issuer mints your security NFT. The same security ID can never be issued twice.

3

Claim interest

On each record date the coupon is recorded for the registered holder. You claim it once as TRXI tokens ($1 each).

4

Redeem to your bank

Request redemption. Tokens are locked, the administrator pays your verified account, then the tokens are burnt.

Safeguards built into the code

  • No duplicate security IDs
  • Transfers only to approved wallets
  • Entitlements never exceed confirmed funding

  • Every action emits a time-stamped event
  • No personal or borrower data on-chain

How amounts are worked out

coupon = face value × yearly rate ÷ payments per year

Example: C$100,000 × 6.00% ÷ 1 = C$6,000.00 = 6,000 TRXI. The holder is read from the ERC-721 contract on the record date.
